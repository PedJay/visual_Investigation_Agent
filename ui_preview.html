History and cultural context

Verified

The stone chariot at the Vitthala Temple complex in Hampi, Karnataka, belongs to a group of monuments listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1986. It appears on the reverse of the ₹50 note that the Reserve Bank of India introduced in August 2017.

Reasoning and evidence

The held note is the decisive clue: its reverse motif is the Hampi stone chariot. The shrine behind it matches that motif (wheels, front elephants, carved superstructure), and the surrounding pillared halls fit the Vitthala complex. No other candidate explains both.
